Counts are the real airway dataset (63,677 genes, 8 samples; genes with fewer than 10 total reads removed). The statistics here are a simplified stand-in for DESeq2: median-of-ratios normalization, then a paired t-test on log2(count+1) across the 4 cell lines with Benjamini-Hochberg correction. Use DESeq2 for real analysis. Only a few well-known genes have symbols shown.
